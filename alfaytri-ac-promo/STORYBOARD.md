## 1. Continuity bible (use the same text in every generation prompt)

Keep these descriptions **word-for-word identical** in every prompt so the people, the AC unit and the home stay the same from scene to scene. If your tool supports reference images or character locking, generate the reference frames first (section 6) and attach them to every scene.

**HOME** — A modern Qatari villa in a residential compound, with sand-beige rendered walls, a flat roof, a dark-wood front door and a small paved driveway with a date palm. Inside: a bright living room with cream walls, a light-grey L-shaped sofa, a low wooden coffee table and sheer white curtains.

**AC UNIT** — A white wall-mounted split AC indoor unit, about 90 cm wide, with a horizontal louvre and a small blue LED display, mounted about 2.2 m high above the sofa. The matching outdoor condenser is a white box with a round fan grille, on wall brackets beside the villa's side wall.

**RESIDENT** — A man in his mid-30s from the Gulf region, wearing a white thobe and no headwear (at home), short dark hair and a neatly trimmed beard.
*(Alternative: a woman in her 30s wearing a black abaya and a loose shayla. Pick one and keep it.)*

**TECHNICIAN A (lead)** — A man in his early 30s with short black hair and a friendly smile.
**TECHNICIAN B** — A man in his late 20s, slightly taller, clean-shaven.
**UNIFORM (both)** — Clean charcoal-grey (#414042) polo shirts with bright orange (#F7901E) shoulder panels and collar trim, charcoal work trousers, grey safety shoes and a tool belt. A small chest patch is left plain in the AI clips; add the real Alfaytri icon there in post (or film real uniforms). In the maintenance close-ups they also wear disposable nitrile gloves.

**VEHICLE** — A clean white compact service van with a bold orange (#F7901E) and charcoal (#414042) diagonal side livery and a blank white panel on the side door, where the real Alfaytri horizontal logo is tracked on in post (`brand/logo/logo_horizontal.png`). **No phone number on the van.**

## 4. Sound and music

- **Music:** light, upbeat acoustic/electronic track, 100–110 BPM, royalty-free or licensed. It starts quiet and slightly muffled (0–10 s), opens up when the maintenance starts (10 s), and resolves on the end card.
- **SFX layers:** cicadas and hot wind (S1), weak AC rattle and remote beeps (S1), van arrival and door (S2), breaker click, water, spray hiss and panel clicks (S3), smooth airflow (S4), a soft "whoosh" logo sting (S5).
- **Mix:** VO at about −14 LUFS integrated for social. Duck the music 6–8 dB under the VO.

## 5. Scene-by-scene video-generation prompts

Written for text/image-to-video models (e.g. Veo, Sora, Runway Gen-4, Kling, Pika, Luma). Generate each shot as a separate 9:16 clip of 3–6 s, then cut them together to the timings above. **Add on-screen text, the logo and the end card in the edit, not in the AI generation**, because AI-generated text and logos come out garbled. Paste the continuity bible descriptions (section 1) where a prompt says `{HOME}`, `{AC UNIT}` and so on.

**Global style suffix (add to every prompt):**
> Photorealistic, cinematic, shot on a full-frame cinema camera with a 35 mm lens, natural lighting, shallow depth of field, realistic skin texture and natural human movement, vertical 9:16, 4K, no on-screen text, no logos, no watermarks, no distorted hands.

**Brand colour cue (add to prompts that show the van or uniforms):** brand colours bright orange #F7901E and charcoal grey #414042.

**Global negative prompt:**
> text, captions, subtitles, logo, watermark, phone number, extra fingers, distorted faces, exaggerated mould, broken AC, sparks, smoke, exposed live wires, water sprayed on electronics, cartoon, CGI look, oversaturated.
