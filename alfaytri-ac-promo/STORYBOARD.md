## 1. Continuity bible (paste word-for-word into every prompt)

Keeping these descriptions identical in every prompt keeps the man, the technician, the room and the AC the same across clips. If your tool supports reference images or character locking, generate the reference stills first (section 6) and attach them to every clip.

**ROOM** — The living room of a modern villa in Qatar: cream walls, a light-grey three-seat fabric sofa, a low walnut coffee table, a beige rug and sheer white curtains over a large window. Bright desert sun is visible outside.

**AC UNIT** — A white wall-mounted split AC indoor unit, about 90 cm wide, with a horizontal louvre and a small blue LED temperature display, mounted about 2.2 m high on the wall above the sofa. Its white outdoor condenser (a box with a round fan grille) sits on wall brackets outside, on the shaded side wall of the villa.

**RESIDENT** — A man in his mid-30s from the Gulf region, wearing a white thobe and no headwear (at home), with short black hair and a neatly trimmed beard. He holds a small white handheld battery fan in scenes 1 and 4.

**TECHNICIAN** — A man in his early 30s, medium build, with short dark hair, clean-shaven, and a calm, friendly manner.
**UNIFORM** — A clean charcoal-grey (#414042) short-sleeve polo with bright orange (#F7901E) shoulder panels and collar trim, charcoal work trousers, grey safety shoes, and a plain chest patch (the real Alfaytri icon is added there in post). He carries a charcoal tool bag with orange trim. For hands-on work he wears blue nitrile gloves.
